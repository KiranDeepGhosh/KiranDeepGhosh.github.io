## About Me
I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Ruhr University Bochum (RUB)](https://www.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/), Germany, working at the Chair for Distributed Cryptography under the supervision of [Prof. Dr. Julian Loss](https://www.julianloss.com/). My research focuses on consensus protocols, digital signatures, and zero-knowledge proofs.

Before joining RUB, I earned an M.Tech. in Cryptology and Security from the [Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata](https://www.isical.ac.in/). For my master's thesis, I collaborated with [COSIC at KU Leuven](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/cosic/), working with [Prof. Bart Preneel](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/cosic/), [Prof. Bimal K. Roy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bimal_Kumar_Roy), and [Dr. Mahdi Sedaghat](https://sedaghat.xyz/).

My academic journey began with a bachelor's degree in Mathematics from [Scottish Church College](https://www.scottishchurch.ac.in/), followed by a master's degree in Mathematics at [Banaras Hindu University](https://www.bhu.ac.in/). My background in mathematics and cryptography drives my interest in building secure cryptographic schemes and proving their security, with a focus on distributed protocols.

## Research Interests

- Digital signatures and it applications.
- Zero-knowledge proofs.
- Distributed computing.
